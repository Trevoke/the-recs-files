* UW-20261014-svb1 Rename a term
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
The record keeper decides that a term is to be spelled differently, its
meaning kept.
** Minimal guarantee
Nothing stands before the record keeper agrees to it.
** Success guarantee
The new spelling stands wherever the old one stood, except in superseded
and retired records and in plans, which stay as written.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper tells the agent the term and its new spelling
*** 2. Agent checks the new spelling against the terms the ubiquitous language already defines
*** 3. Agent finds every occurrence of the term, its stem and its synonyms, and counts them
*** 4. Agent shows the record keeper the count and each sentence with its replacement
*** 5. Record keeper agrees
*** 6. Agent replaces every occurrence in one pass, and counts again
** Extensions
*** 2a. The new spelling is a term the ubiquitous language already defines
**** 2a1. Agent names that term and proposes other candidates
**** 2a2. Record keeper agrees to one; continue at 3
*** 3a. Other words name the same concept as the term
**** 3a1. Agent shows each with its occurrences; the record keeper decides which are the same concept, and those are renamed with it
*** 4a. Record keeper rewords a replacement
**** 4a1. Agent redrafts; back to 4
*** 4b. Record keeper declines
**** 4b1. Nothing is written; the use case ends
*** 6a. The count finds an occurrence not shown at 4
**** 6a1. Agent shows it to the record keeper; back to 5
