* UW-7 Realize a change to the system
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
The record keeper wants the system to do something it does not yet do.
** Minimal guarantee
No record change stands before the record keeper agrees to it, and nothing
in the realization rests on a proposed or rejected record.
** Success guarantee
Every decision the change needed stands in a record, and the realization
does what those records say, checked against them.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper tells the agent what the system should do
*** 2. Agent reads the records that bear on it, and the project's realization modalities from its AGENTS.md
*** 3. Agent and record keeper settle the change, and each decision that belongs in a record is made a record change (UW-1: Create a record; UW-2: Supersede a record)
*** 4. Agent writes a plan, each step citing the records it realizes
*** 5. Agent realizes the plan in the project's realization modalities, checking each step against the records it cites
*** 6. Agent shows the record keeper the realization and how it was checked
*** 7. Record keeper agrees
** Extensions
*** 1a. Record keeper names a user workflow, or a step or extension of it, that the change starts from
**** 1a1. Agent reads that user workflow first, and frames the change from it; continue at 2
*** 2a. The project's AGENTS.md names no realization modality
**** 2a1. Record keeper chooses one, and the agent names it in AGENTS.md; continue at 3
*** 5a. A check fails
**** 5a1. Agent finds the cause before changing the realization; back to 5
*** 5b. Realizing the plan turns up a decision no record holds
**** 5b1. Agent raises it as a record change (UW-1: Create a record); once it stands, back to 5
*** 7a. Record keeper declines the realization
**** 7a1. Agent and record keeper settle what differs; back to 3
