* UW-2 Supersede a record
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Standing
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
The record keeper decides that what a standing record decides has changed.
** Minimal guarantee
Nothing is written before the record keeper agrees to it.
** Success guarantee
The successor is written and listed in its projection; the old record's
status names its successor and is the only change to it; every citation of
the old record outside a plan points at the successor.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper tells the agent what has changed in what the record decides
*** 2. Agent drafts the successor, and finds every sentence the change falsifies, with the wording that replaces it
*** 3. Agent shows the record keeper the successor, the old record's new status, and the falsified sentences with their replacements
*** 4. Record keeper agrees
*** 5. Agent writes the successor and its line in the projection, sets the old record's status, and sweeps the old record's citations
** Extensions
*** 1a. Record keeper asks for an in-place edit instead
**** 1a1. Agent assesses whether the record's intent is kept, and how it changes if not
**** 1a2. Record keeper decides the intent is kept: Clarify a record (UW-4: Clarify a record); the use case ends
**** 1a3. Record keeper decides the intent changes; continue at 2
*** 2a. The change falsifies only part of what the record decides
**** 2a1. Agent drafts two successors, the half that holds carried over in its own words, and assigns each citation to the successor whose half it meant
*** 2b. The change replaces several records at once
**** 2b1. Agent drafts one successor that supersedes all of them
*** 2c. The successor needs a term the ubiquitous language does not define
**** 2c1. Agent proposes candidate words and waits
**** 2c2. Record keeper agrees to one; continue at 2
*** 3a. Record keeper rewords the draft
**** 3a1. Agent redrafts; back to 3
*** 3b. Record keeper declines
**** 3b1. Nothing is written; the use case ends
*** 5a. The sweep finds a falsified sentence not shown at 3
**** 5a1. Agent shows it to the record keeper; back to 4
