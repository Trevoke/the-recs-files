* UW-4 Clarify a record
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
The record keeper finds that a standing record's wording falls short of its
intent.
** Minimal guarantee
Nothing stands before the record keeper agrees to it.
** Success guarantee
The record's wording, and its title where the record keeper agreed to a
new one, are changed in place; its number and intent are unchanged; every
citation of it outside a plan carries its title.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper asks the agent for an in-place edit of a record
*** 2. Agent drafts the new wording, and assesses whether the record's intent is kept, and how it changes if not, naming each citation that would read wrong after the edit
*** 3. Agent shows the record keeper the draft and the assessment, with the rule: if the intent doesn't change, then it might be the same record
*** 4. Record keeper decides the intent is kept, and agrees to the wording
*** 5. Agent writes the new wording into the record
** Extensions
*** 2a. The edit gives the record a new title
**** 2a1. Agent finds every citation of the record outside a plan, each with the new title in place of the old; continue at 3
*** 4a. Record keeper decides the intent changes
**** 4a1. Supersede a record (UW-2: Supersede a record); the use case ends
*** 4b. Record keeper rewords the draft
**** 4b1. Agent redrafts; back to 3
*** 4c. Record keeper declines
**** 4c1. Nothing is written; the use case ends
