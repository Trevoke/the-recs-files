* UW-6 Redefine a term
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
The record keeper decides that a term's meaning changes and its spelling
stays.
** Minimal guarantee
Nothing stands before the record keeper agrees to it.
** Success guarantee
The term's record states its new meaning; every falsified sentence outside
superseded and retired records and plans is replaced, and every other
occurrence stands.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper tells the agent the term and its new meaning
*** 2. Agent drafts the successor to the record that defines the term
*** 3. Agent names every other term whose meaning the change moves
*** 4. Agent finds every occurrence of each term, its stem and its synonyms, and counts them
*** 5. Agent writes the rule that tells a false sentence from a true one, and gives each occurrence a verdict: its replacement, or stands and why
*** 6. Agent shows the record keeper the successor, the rule and every verdict
*** 7. Record keeper agrees
*** 8. Agent supersedes the term's record, writes every replacement in one pass, and counts again
** Extensions
*** 1a. The meaning stays, and only the definition's wording falls short
**** 1a1. Clarify a record ([[./uw-4-clarify-a-record.org][UW-4: Clarify a record]]); the use case ends
*** 6a. Record keeper rewords the successor, the rule or a verdict
**** 6a1. Agent redrafts; back to 6
*** 7a. Record keeper declines
**** 7a1. Nothing is written; the use case ends
*** 8a. The count finds a false sentence not shown at 6
**** 8a1. Agent shows it to the record keeper; back to 7
