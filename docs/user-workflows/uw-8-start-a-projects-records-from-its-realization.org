* UW-8 Start a project's records from its realization
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
A project has a realization and keeps no records, or only scattered docs.
** Minimal guarantee
No record change stands before the record keeper agrees to it, and the
realization is unchanged.
** Success guarantee
The project's vision, its core user workflow, the workflows in active
development and the records they touch stand, and its AGENTS.md names its
projections and its realization modalities.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper asks the agent to start the project's records
*** 2. Agent reads what the project holds: its AGENTS.md, docs, history and realization
*** 3. Record keeper chooses the project's realization modalities ([[../features/f-11-the-record-keeper-chooses-a-projects-realization-modalities.org][F-11: The record keeper chooses a project's realization modalities]])
*** 4. Agent and record keeper name the domain, its bounded contexts, the core user workflow and the workflows in active development
*** 5. Category by category, the agent shows titles, then bodies in batches, and each agreed batch is created ([[./uw-1-create-a-record.org][UW-1: Create a record]])
*** 6. Agent drafts the project's AGENTS.md, naming its projections and realization modalities, and shows every line it adds, changes or removes
*** 7. Record keeper agrees
*** 8. Agent lists the changes to the realization that the records now call for
** Extensions
*** 2a. The project's AGENTS.md already names its realization modalities
**** 2a1. Agent shows them to the record keeper to confirm; continue at 4
*** 7a. Record keeper rewords a line
**** 7a1. Agent redrafts; back to 6
