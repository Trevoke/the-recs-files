* UW-20261028-838j Answer a question the agent raises
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: Subfunction
:END:
** Stakeholders & Interests
- Record keeper: answers from their own records, with the question's context
  given and bounded, and a concrete case of it in front of them, so they need
  not work out which records, which parts of them, or which situation the
  agent means.
- Agent: gets an answer it can act on, resting on the records it cited.
** Trigger
The agent comes to a situation that needs the record keeper's decision, in a
brainstorm, a clarification or a conversation, or the record keeper asks
about one.
** Minimal guarantee
The agent acts on nothing the question leaves to the record keeper before
they answer.
** Success guarantee
The record keeper decides the question from the records cited, and the agent
knows which records and sections the answer rests on.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Agent finds the user workflow the situation is in, or each of them where it is in more than one, and the step or extension of each that applies
*** 2. Agent finds every other record that bears on the situation, and the sections of each that do
*** 3. Agent cites each workflow and its step or extension, then each record that bears, with what each cited section says ([[../features/f-20261028-1mkt-the-agent-cites-a-record.org][F-20261028-1mkt: The agent cites a record]])
*** 4. Agent states the situation in the words the records have for it
*** 5. Agent gives at least one example of the situation, concrete enough to show what the decision is about
*** 6. Agent asks the question, and waits
*** 7. Record keeper answers
** Extensions
*** 1a. No user workflow holds the situation
**** 1a1. Agent says so, and frames the question from the records that bear; continue at 2
*** 4a. The records have no word for the situation
**** 4a1. Agent proposes candidate words and waits for the record keeper to agree to one; continue at 4
*** 7a. The answer needs a record change
**** 7a1. Agent raises it as a record change ([[./uw-20261014-8c8f-create-a-record.org][UW-20261014-8c8f: Create a record]]; [[./uw-20261014-wewg-supersede-a-record.org][UW-20261014-wewg: Supersede a record]]; [[./uw-20261014-q1yj-clarify-a-record.org][UW-20261014-q1yj: Clarify a record]])
*** 7b. Record keeper cannot answer from what the question gives
**** 7b1. Agent brings the record, the section or the example that is missing; back to 6
