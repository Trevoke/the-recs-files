* UW-1 Create a record
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Standing
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
Something worth recording surfaces: a decision, a definition, a workflow, a
feature, or the exact text the system gives on a refusal or a failure.
** Minimal guarantee
Nothing is written before the record keeper agrees to it.
** Success guarantee
The record is written in a file of its own and listed in its category's
projection, and every sentence it falsifies is replaced.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper tells the agent what is to be recorded
*** 2. Agent places it in a category, and searches the standing records for one that already decides it
*** 3. Agent drafts the record in its category's form, and finds every sentence the record falsifies, with the wording that replaces it
*** 4. Agent shows the record keeper the draft and the falsified sentences with their replacements
*** 5. Record keeper agrees
*** 6. Agent writes the record, its line in the projection, and the replacements
** Extensions
*** 2a. A standing record already decides it
**** 2a1. Supersede a record (UW-2: Supersede a record); the use case ends
*** 2b. The placement tests do not settle the category
**** 2b1. Agent shows what each test found, and the record keeper picks the category
*** 3a. The record needs a term the ubiquitous language does not define
**** 3a1. Agent proposes candidate words, built from existing words first, and waits
**** 3a2. Record keeper agrees to one; continue at 3
*** 4a. Record keeper rewords the draft
**** 4a1. Agent redrafts; back to 4
*** 4b. Record keeper declines
**** 4b1. Nothing is written; the use case ends
*** 6a. Writing turns up a falsified sentence not shown at 4
**** 6a1. Agent shows it to the record keeper; back to 5
