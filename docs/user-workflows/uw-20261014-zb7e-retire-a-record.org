* UW-20261014-zb7e Retire a record
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Record keeper
:SCOPE: The recs files
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
The record keeper decides that what a standing record decides no longer
holds, and nothing replaces it.
** Minimal guarantee
Nothing stands before the record keeper agrees to it.
** Success guarantee
The record's status is Retired, with the reason in a sentence, and that is
the only change to it; its line is removed from its projection; every
citation of it outside a plan is removed.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Record keeper tells the agent which record no longer holds, and why
*** 2. Agent finds every citation of the record, and every sentence its retirement falsifies, with the wording that replaces it
*** 3. Agent shows the record keeper the new status with its reason, and the falsified sentences with their replacements
*** 4. Record keeper agrees
*** 5. Agent sets the record's status, removes its line from the projection and its citations, and writes the replacements
** Extensions
*** 1a. Something replaces the record
**** 1a1. Supersede a record ([[./uw-20261014-wewg-supersede-a-record.org][UW-20261014-wewg: Supersede a record]]); the use case ends
*** 3a. Record keeper rewords the reason or a replacement
**** 3a1. Agent redrafts; back to 3
*** 3b. Record keeper declines
**** 3b1. Nothing is written; the use case ends
*** 5a. The sweep finds a falsified sentence not shown at 3
**** 5a1. Agent shows it to the record keeper; back to 4
