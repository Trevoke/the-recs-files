#+TITLE: Vision

* What the recs files are for
The recs files are a set of skills that instruct an agent to keep the truth
of a system in records. Each record belongs to a category, and each
category's records are read through a projection named after it. The
system need not be code. The recs files are reused across projects; each
project's own facts live in its AGENTS.md.

* Who they serve
The record keeper: the person or team whose job is to manage a project's
records, whether a developer or a product owner. Records change only at the
record keeper's direction. The agent is what the skills instruct.

* The domain
A project's records: short, numbered statements of what its system does and
why. The records are the truth for the system; the record keeper is the
truth for the records.

* Bounded contexts
- Record management :: making record changes (creating, superseding,
  retiring a record) and sweeping a word through the project.
- Record realization :: taking a project from brainstorming to a
  realization of its records, in code, skill text or other work, reading
  and citing its records along the way.

* Open questions
- Record realization's language is adopted beyond UL-22: Realization and
  UL-23: Record realization only one term at a time. Its skills are largely
  borrowed and written for code alone.
