* ADR-7 The agent speaks in the ubiquitous language
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
The record keeper and the system meet through the agent. A word the agent
coins, or a synonym it drifts to, is one the record keeper never agreed to
and no record defines.
** Decision
We will have the skills tell the agent to speak in the ubiquitous
language: in what it says to the record keeper, in what it writes into
records, and in the names it gives in the system.
** Consequences
The ubiquitous language is a clear thread for the conversation between
the record keeper and the system, through the agent. A word the agent
needs and the ubiquitous language lacks is proposed before it is used.
