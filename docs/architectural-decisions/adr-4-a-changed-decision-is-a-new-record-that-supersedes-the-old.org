* ADR-4 A changed decision is a new record that supersedes the old
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
A record's text is what the record keeper agreed to, and records, code
and plans cite it. An edit in place changes what each citation meant
without anyone agreeing to the new meaning, and loses what the record once
decided. Some edits only bring a record's wording closer to its intent.
Nygard's records are never edited; a superseded one stays, and points at
its successor.
** Decision
We will make every change to what a record decides as a new record that
supersedes the old, and never reuse a record's number. We will edit a
record in place only as a clarification, when the record keeper decides
that its intent is kept.
** Consequences
Every decision the project has made stays readable, beside what replaced
it. A supersede sweeps the old record's citations to its successors. A
clarification may change a record's title, and its citations then carry the
new one. Whether the intent is kept is the record keeper's call; the agent
assesses it and does not decide it.
