* ADR-5 A plan keeps the citations it was written with
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
A plan is the work as planned against the records standing when it was
written. A sweep repoints the citations of a superseded record, removes
those of a retired one, and replaces a renamed or redefined term.
** Decision
We will leave every word and every citation in a plan as written when a
sweep runs.
** Consequences
A plan reads later as it read when it was made, so its reasoning can be
followed. A plan may cite a withdrawn record or use a word since renamed;
its reader follows the withdrawn record's status to its successor. Every
sweep skips plans.
