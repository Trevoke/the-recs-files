* ADR-20261015-70tz A record change is one commit of its own
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
A record change touches the record, its line in its projection, and every
sentence it falsifies, often across several files. The truth for the
system changes when the change is written, and the project's history is
read from its commits. Other work, such as code, skill text or a plan, is
often in progress in the same worktree at the same time.
** Decision
We will commit each record change, with its projection's line and the
replacements of its falsified sentences, in one commit that holds nothing
else. We will commit a proposal in a commit of its own, before the one that
accepts or rejects it.
** Consequences
Every change to the truth has a single, clear point in time, found in the
history by its commit. A record change never rides along with code; the
code that follows it is committed after it. Files are staged by name, so
work in progress stays out of the commit.
