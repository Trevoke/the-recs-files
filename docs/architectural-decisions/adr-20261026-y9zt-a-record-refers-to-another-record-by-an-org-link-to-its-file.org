* ADR-20261026-y9zt A record refers to another record by an org link to its file
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
A record refers to other records: a successor to the records it supersedes,
a workflow's extension to the workflow it goes to, a decision to the
decisions it rests on. Written as plain text, a record name wrapped across two lines is found by
no search for it, and several record names on one property line have nothing to
part them: a title holds commas. An org link ends where its brackets close,
and opens the file it names.
** Decision
We will refer to a record, wherever another record refers to it, by an org link
to its file, relative to the referring record's file, the link's description its
identifier, the section where one is meant, and its title, as a citation
carries them:
[[./adr-20261015-nz3v-a-changed-decision-is-a-new-record-that-supersedes-the-old.org][ADR-20261015-nz3v: A changed decision is a new record that supersedes the old]]
** Consequences
A successor's =:SUPERSEDES:= holds one link for each record it supersedes,
a comma and a space between links, on one line, and a superseded record's =:STATUS:=
refers to its successor by a link. A clarification that gives a record a new
title renames its file, and every link to it changes with its citations. A
sweep of a withdrawn record's identifier finds the links to it as it finds
its citations. A projection keeps its own line form.
