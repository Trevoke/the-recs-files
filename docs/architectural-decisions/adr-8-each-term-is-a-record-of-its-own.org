* ADR-8 Each term is a record of its own
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
The ubiquitous language was kept as one file of unnumbered terms. A
redefinition changes what a term means, and every sentence that uses the
term reads differently after it.
[[./adr-4-a-changed-decision-is-a-new-record-that-supersedes-the-old.org][ADR-4: A changed decision is a new record that supersedes the old]]
makes every change to what a record decides a new record; a term without a
number cannot be superseded, so redefining one needed an exception to
[[./adr-4-a-changed-decision-is-a-new-record-that-supersedes-the-old.org][ADR-4: A changed decision is a new record that supersedes the old]].
** Decision
We will keep each term, and each bounded context, as a numbered ubiquitous
language record, UL-n, in a file of its own and titled with the term. The
projection lists the terms grouped under their bounded contexts.
** Consequences
A redefinition supersedes the term's record like any other record, with
no exception. A term is cited by number and title, as
[[../ubiquitous-language/ul-2-record-keeper.org][UL-2: Record keeper]].
A rename that keeps a term's meaning is a clarification of its title. The
ubiquitous language takes as many files as it has terms.
