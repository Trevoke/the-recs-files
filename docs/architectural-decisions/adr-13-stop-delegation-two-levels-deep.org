* ADR-13 Stop delegation two levels deep
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Proposed
:END:
** Context
A subagent that can dispatch subagents can nest without end, each level
spending more than the one above it.
** Decision
We will let the agent and its subagents dispatch subagents, and withhold the
delegate harness capability (ADR-10: Render each harness's package from
capability markers and a harness profile) from a subagent's subagents. Where
the subagent tool is the recs files' own, each subagent it spawns carries its
depth in =TRF_DELEGATE_DEPTH=, and the tool is not offered at depth 2.
** Consequences
A subagent's subagent does its work itself. A harness whose subagent tool is
its own keeps its own limit; this decision binds the recs files' tool.
