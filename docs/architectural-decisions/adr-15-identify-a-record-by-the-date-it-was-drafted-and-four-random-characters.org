* ADR-15 Identify a record by the date it was drafted and four random characters
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
A record's
[[../ubiquitous-language/ul-25-identifier.org][UL-25: Identifier]]
was the next number its category had not used, which only someone who
sees every record of the category can know. Two branches drafting at once
take the same number, and records carried into another project take
numbers that project already uses. An agent asked for random characters
repeats itself.
** Decision
We will give each record the identifier =<PREFIX>-<YYYYMMDD>-<xxxx>=: its
category's prefix, the UTC date it was drafted, and four characters of
lowercase Crockford base32, =0123456789abcdefghjkmnpqrstvwxyz=, drawn by a
command the agent runs. A record's file is named by its lowercased
identifier, a hyphen and its slug.
** Consequences
A projection in identifier order lists its records by the date they were
drafted, and those of one day in the order of their characters. Two
records of one category drafted on one day share an identifier once in
1,048,576 draws, and a draw that names an existing record file is drawn
again. This project's numbered records are re-identified, each dated by
the commit that first added its file. A project that adopts the recs files
keeps its numbered records until its record keeper directs otherwise, and
the agent reads both forms. The identifier departs from Nygard's
sequential numbering, and keeps his rule that a record's identity is
never reused.
