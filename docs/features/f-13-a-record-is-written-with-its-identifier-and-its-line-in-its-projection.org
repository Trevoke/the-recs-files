* F-13 A record is written with its identifier and its line in its projection
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:SUPERSEDES: [[./f-4-a-record-is-written-with-its-line-in-its-projection.org][F-4: A record is written with its line in its projection]] (§1 → §1; §2 → §2; §3 → §4; §4 → §5; §5 → §6; §6 → §7; §7 → §8)
:END:
1. When the agent drafts a new record, the agent shall write it as
   proposed, in a file of its own, with its line under its projection's
   Proposed heading.
2. When the agent drafts a new record, the agent shall give it the
   [[../ubiquitous-language/ul-25-identifier.org][UL-25: Identifier]]
   made of its category's prefix, the UTC date of the draft, and four
   characters drawn at random from lowercase Crockford base32.
   - The one where an architectural decision drafted on 26 October 2026
     is ADR-20261026-kzx5.
3. If a drawn identifier names a record file in its category's
   directory, then the agent shall draw again.
4. The agent shall give a record's file, its heading and its line in the
   projection the same title.
5. The agent shall never reuse a record's identifier.
6. When a proposed record is accepted, the agent shall move its line out
   from under its projection's Proposed heading.
7. When a record change is agreed, the agent shall mark the record
   accepted and write every falsified sentence's replacement in one pass.
8. When a record is rejected or withdrawn, the agent shall remove its line
   from its projection.
