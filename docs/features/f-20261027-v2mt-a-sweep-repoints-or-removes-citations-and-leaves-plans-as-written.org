* F-20261027-v2mt A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:SUPERSEDES: [[./f-20261015-egtj-a-sweep-repoints-or-removes-citations-and-leaves-plans-as-written.org][F-20261015-egtj: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written]]
:END:
1. When a supersede is agreed, the agent shall repoint every citation of
   the old record outside a plan to the successor that carries what the
   citation meant.
2. Where a citation refers to a section of a superseded record, the agent shall
   repoint it to the successor's sections that carry what the citing site
   rests on, read against the successor.
3. When a retirement is agreed, the agent shall remove every citation of
   the record outside a plan, and reword the sentence that held it so that
   it still reads true.
4. When a clarification gives a record a new title, the agent shall carry
   the new title into every citation of the record outside a plan.
5. The agent shall change no word and no citation in a plan.
6. When the agent has written a sweep, the agent shall search again for the
   record's identifier, and show the record keeper any citation that remains
   outside a plan.
7. If the agent cannot tell which of a successor's sections carry what a
   citation of a section rests on, then the agent shall show the record
   keeper that citation before repointing it.
