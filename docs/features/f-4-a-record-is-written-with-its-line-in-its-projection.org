* F-4 A record is written with its line in its projection
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Proposed
:END:
1. When the agent drafts a new record, the agent shall write it as
   proposed, in a file of its own, with its line under its projection's
   Proposed heading.
2. The agent shall give a new record the next number its category has not
   used.
3. The agent shall give a record's file, its heading and its line in the
   projection the same title.
4. The agent shall never reuse a record's number.
5. When a proposed record is accepted, the agent shall move its line out
   from under its projection's Proposed heading.
6. When a record change is agreed, the agent shall mark the record
   accepted and write every falsified sentence's replacement in one pass.
