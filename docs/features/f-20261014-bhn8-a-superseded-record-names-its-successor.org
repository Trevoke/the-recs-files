* F-20261014-bhn8 A superseded record names its successor
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Superseded by [[./f-20261027-jtb9-a-superseded-record-refers-to-its-successor.org][F-20261027-jtb9: A superseded record refers to its successor]]
:END:
1. When a supersede is agreed, the agent shall set the old record's status
   to name its successor by identifier and title.
2. When the agent drafts a successor, the agent shall name, in its
   :SUPERSEDES: property, every record it supersedes by identifier and title.
3. Where the superseded record numbers its sections, the agent shall map,
   in the successor, each old section to the successor's sections that
   carry it, or to none.
4. When a supersede is agreed, the agent shall change nothing in the old
   record but its status.
5. When a change falsifies only part of what a record decides, the agent
   shall supersede it with two successors, the part that holds carried over
   in its own words.
6. When a change replaces several records, the agent shall supersede them
   all with one successor.
