* F-20261015-egtj A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
1. When a supersede is agreed, the agent shall repoint every citation of
   the old record outside a plan to the successor that carries what the
   citation meant.
2. Where a citation names a section of a superseded record, the agent shall
   repoint it by the successor's section map.
3. When a retirement is agreed, the agent shall remove every citation of
   the record outside a plan, and reword the sentence that held it so that
   it still reads true.
4. When a clarification gives a record a new title, the agent shall carry
   the new title into every citation of the record outside a plan.
5. The agent shall change no word and no citation in a plan.
6. When the agent has written a sweep, the agent shall search again for the
   record's identifier, and show the record keeper any citation that remains
   outside a plan.
