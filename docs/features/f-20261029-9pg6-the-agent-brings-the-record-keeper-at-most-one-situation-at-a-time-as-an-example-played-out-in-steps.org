* F-20261029-9pg6 The agent brings the record keeper at most one situation at a time, as an example played out in steps
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Proposed
:SUPERSEDES: [[./f-20261029-sn02-the-agent-brings-the-record-keeper-one-situation-at-a-time-as-an-example-played-out-in-steps.org][F-20261029-sn02: The agent brings the record keeper one situation at a time, as an example played out in steps]]
:END:
1. When the agent brings the record keeper a situation that needs their
   decision, the agent shall cite the user workflow the situation is in, or
   each of them where it is in more than one, with the step or extension of
   each that applies.
   - The one where the agent, unsure whether a clarification keeps a
     record's intent, first cites the step of
     [[../user-workflows/uw-20261014-q1yj-clarify-a-record.org][UW-20261014-q1yj: Clarify a record]]
     where the record keeper decides it.
2. If no user workflow holds the situation, then the agent shall say so.
   - The one where the record keeper asks whether two records use a word
     the same way, and the agent says that no user workflow holds the
     question.
3. When the agent brings the record keeper a situation, the agent shall
   cite each other record that bears on it, with the sections of each that
   do.
4. The agent shall state a situation it brings the record keeper in the
   words the records have for it.
5. When the agent brings the record keeper a situation, the agent shall
   give at least one example of it: a phrase that names it, starting "The
   one where", then numbered steps, each one thing that an actor or the
   system does, with concrete actors, values and dates.
   - The one where a borrower's renewal is refused because another
     borrower reserved the book an hour after the loan, played out in five
     steps from the loan on 1 October at 10:00 to the refusal on 10 October.
6. When the agent brings the record keeper a choice between options, the
   agent shall play out one example under each option, with the same
   actors, values and dates.
   - The one where a fourth renewal of the same loan is refused under a
     fixed limit of three, and extended under a limit the library sets at
     five.
7. When the agent asks the record keeper about a situation, the agent shall
   point the question to the step of the example whose outcome no record
   settles.
8. When the agent has asked the record keeper about a situation, the agent
   shall act on nothing the question leaves to them before they answer.
9. If the record keeper cannot answer from what the agent brought, then the
   agent shall bring the record, the section or the example that is
   missing.
10. When the agent finds more than one situation that needs the record
    keeper's decision, the agent shall bring the one the record keeper's
    question is about, or, where it is about none of them, the one the work
    stops on.
    - The one where the record keeper asks for the wording of a 7-day loan
      period for new books, and the agent brings the loan period.
11. When the agent finds more than one situation that needs the record
    keeper's decision, the agent shall list each situation it does not
    bring as open, in one sentence that says what it is about, with no
    example.
    - The one where the agent, having brought the 7-day loan period, lists
      as open that no record says what makes a book new.
12. When the agent answers a question of the record keeper's that is about
    no situation that needs their decision, the agent shall list as open
    each situation it finds that the work does not stop on, and bring none
    of them.
    - The one where the record keeper asks what the library refuses when a
      borrower renews a loan, and the agent gives the two refusals, then
      lists as open that no step of the renewal workflow reaches the
      renewal limit.
