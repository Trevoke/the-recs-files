* F-6 A retired record gives its reason
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
1. When a retirement is agreed, the agent shall set the record's status to
   Retired, with the reason in one sentence.
2. When a retirement is agreed, the agent shall change nothing in the
   retired record but its status.
3. If something replaces the record, then the agent shall propose
   superseding it instead of retiring it.
