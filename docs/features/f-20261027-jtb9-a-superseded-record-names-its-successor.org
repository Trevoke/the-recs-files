* F-20261027-jtb9 A superseded record names its successor
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Proposed
:SUPERSEDES: [[./f-20261014-bhn8-a-superseded-record-names-its-successor.org][F-20261014-bhn8: A superseded record names its successor]]
:END:
1. When a supersede is agreed, the agent shall set the old record's status
   to name its successor by identifier and title.
2. When the agent drafts a successor, the agent shall name, in its
   :SUPERSEDES: property, every record it supersedes by identifier and title.
3. When a supersede is agreed, the agent shall change nothing in the old
   record but its status.
4. When a change falsifies only part of what a record decides, the agent
   shall supersede it with two successors, the part that holds carried over
   in its own words.
5. When a change replaces several records, the agent shall supersede them
   all with one successor.
