* F-20261028-1mkt The agent cites a record
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:SUPERSEDES: [[./f-20261027-q7eh-the-agent-cites-a-record-to-the-record-keeper-in-its-titles-exact-words-says-beneath-it-what-each-cited-section-says-then-names-it-again-by-its-identifier.org][F-20261027-q7eh: The agent cites a record to the record keeper in its title's exact words, says beneath it what each cited section says, then names it again by its identifier]]
:END:
1. When the agent first refers to a record in a reply to the record keeper,
   the agent shall cite it, giving its record name as the record's heading
   carries it.
   - The one where the agent, about to open the feature on sweeps, writes
     that it will open
     `F-20261027-v2mt: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`.
2. When the agent refers again to a record that the reply has cited, the
   agent shall refer to it in words that make plain to the record keeper
   which cited record is meant: its citation, its title, or a description
   of it.
   - The one where the agent, having cited
     `F-20261027-v2mt: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     earlier in its reply, says that
     `A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     leaves plans as written.
   - The one where the agent, having cited
     `ADR-20261015-nz3v: A changed decision is a new record that supersedes the old`,
     says that the decision keeps a superseded record's text as written.
3. When the agent refers again to a section that the reply has cited, the
   agent shall refer to it in words that make plain to the record keeper
   which cited section is meant: its citation, its record's title followed
   by the section, or a description of it.
   - The one where the agent, having cited
     `F-20261027-v2mt §3: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     earlier in its reply, says that
     `A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written §3`
     removes the citations of a retired record.
   - The one where the agent, having cited
     `UW-20261028-838j §3: Answer a question the agent raises`, refers to it
     again as the step where the agent cites the records that bear.
4. When the agent first refers to a section of a record in a reply to the
   record keeper, the agent shall give that section in a citation.
   - The one where the agent, having cited
     `F-20261027-v2mt: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     whole, comes to its third requirement and cites
     `F-20261027-v2mt §3: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`.
5. Where the agent refers to sections of a record to the record keeper, the
   agent shall refer to every section of that record a sentence means
   together, in one citation, after the identifier, each written out, in
   the record's order.
   - The one where the agent cites the fourth requirement of F-20261027-v2mt as
     `F-20261027-v2mt §4: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`.
   - The one where the agent cites the second and seventh requirements of
     F-20261027-v2mt as one citation,
     `F-20261027-v2mt §2, §7: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`.
6. When the agent first refers to a section of a record in a reply to the
   record keeper, the agent shall set directly under that line, for each
   section it refers to, either a list item of its own giving § and the
   section's number, then what that section says, or a quote of that section.
   - The one where the agent cites
     `F-20261027-v2mt §3: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     and writes under it the list item "- §3: a retirement removes each citation".
   - The one where the agent cites
     `F-20261027-v2mt §1, §3: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     and writes under it the list items "- §1: a supersede repoints each
     citation to the successor" and "- §3: a retirement removes each citation".
   - The one where the agent quotes the second requirement of F-20261027-jtb9
     in a code block under
     `F-20261027-jtb9 §2: A superseded record refers to its successor`,
     and writes no other line for §2.
7. The agent shall set in backticks each citation it gives the record
   keeper, and each title it refers to a record by.
8. The agent shall write each citation it gives the record keeper with no
   line break inside it.
9. Where a record the agent speaks of is not yet on disk, the agent shall
   refer to its sections by what they say, without numbers.
   - The one where the agent tells the record keeper that a successor not
     yet written would add a requirement that notifies the borrower.
10. When the agent presents text as a record's own words, the agent shall
    give it exactly as the file holds it, under the line that refers to what
    it quotes, set in a code block.
    - The one where the agent quotes the second requirement of
      F-20261027-jtb9 in a code block under
      `F-20261027-jtb9 §2: A superseded record refers to its successor`.
    - The one where the agent writes, in its own sentence, that a sweep
      leaves plans as written, in the words of F-20261027-v2mt, and sets no
      quote.
