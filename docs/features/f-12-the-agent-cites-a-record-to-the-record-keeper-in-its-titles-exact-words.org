* F-12 The agent cites a record to the record keeper in its title's exact words
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
1. When the agent cites a record to the record keeper, the agent shall give
   the record's identifier and its title as the record's heading carries it.
2. Where a citation to the record keeper names a section, the agent shall
   name one section in it, after the identifier and before the title.
   - The one where the agent cites the fourth requirement of F-7 as
     `F-7 §4: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`.
   - The one where the agent cites the second and third requirements of F-7
     as two citations, `F-7 §2: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`
     and `F-7 §3: A sweep repoints or removes citations, and leaves plans as written`.
3. The agent shall set each citation it gives the record keeper in
   backticks.
4. The agent shall write each citation it gives the record keeper with no
   line break inside it.
