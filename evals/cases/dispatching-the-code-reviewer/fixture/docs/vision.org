#+TITLE: Vision

* What the apiary log is for
The apiary log keeps a beekeeper's record of each hive: when it was
inspected, stored as the local time it was taken, what was found, and what
was done.

* Who it serves
Hobby beekeepers who keep a handful of hives on one or more stands.

* Bounded contexts
- Hive keeping :: hives, their stands and their inspections.
