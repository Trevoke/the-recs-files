* UW-1 Renew a loan before its due date
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Borrower
:SCOPE: Circulation
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
A borrower wants to keep a book past its due date.
** Minimal guarantee
A loan's due date changes only on a renewal the library allows.
** Success guarantee
The loan is due one loan period after the renewal date.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Borrower asks to renew a loan
*** 2. Library finds no reservation on the book ([[../features/f-1-a-borrower-renews-a-loan-for-one-more-loan-period-unless-the-book-is-reserved.org][F-1: A borrower renews a loan for one more loan period unless the book is reserved]])
*** 3. Library sets the new due date
*** 4. Borrower sees the new due date
** Extensions
*** 2a. The book is reserved
**** 2a1. Library refuses the renewal with [[../message-catalog/msg-1-a-reserved-book-cannot-be-renewed.org][MSG-1: A reserved book cannot be renewed]]; the use case ends
