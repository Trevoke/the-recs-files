#+TITLE: Vision

* What the plot register is for
The plot register allocates a community garden's plots to its gardeners,
one season at a time, and keeps the waiting list for plots.

* Who it serves
The garden's coordinator, who allocates the plots, and the gardeners who
hold them or wait for one.

* Bounded contexts
- Allocation :: plots, the gardeners who hold them, and the waiting list.
