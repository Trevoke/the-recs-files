* ADR-2 A hive is identified by its stand and position
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:END:
** Context
Beekeepers name hives by where they stand: "stand B, third from the left".
Hives are rarely moved once placed.
** Decision
We will identify a hive by its stand letter and its position on the stand.
** Consequences
Moving a hive changes its identity, so a move is recorded as the old hive
ending and a new one starting.
