* UW-2 Give up a plot
:PROPERTIES:
:STATUS: Accepted
:ACTOR: Gardener
:SCOPE: The plot register
:LEVEL: User goal
:END:
** Trigger
A gardener holding a plot no longer wants it before the season ends.
** Minimal guarantee
The gardener keeps the plot until the coordinator records it given up.
** Success guarantee
The gardener holds no plot, and the plot is held by the next gardener on
the waiting list, or is free.
** Main success scenario
*** 1. Gardener tells the coordinator they are giving up their plot
*** 2. Coordinator records the plot given up ([[../features/f-2-a-gardener-can-give-up-a-plot.org][F-2: A gardener can give up a plot]])
** Extensions
*** 2a. The waiting list is empty
**** 2a1. The plot stays free; the use case ends
