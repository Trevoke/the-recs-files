# Receiving a review of skill text

Carries `UW-20261015-nkb5 §5: Realize a change to the system`: a step is
checked against the records it cites, and so is the review of it.

**Core principle:** a finding is right because the record it cites says so,
and for no other reason. Open the record before acting.

## For each finding

1. Open the record section the finding cites, from disk.
2. Does the section say what the finding says it says? If not, answer with
   the section quoted, and change nothing.
3. Does the sentence break it? A sentence the reviewer calls fitted to a
   case is measured against the records only: where every situation it
   names is one a record names, it stands, and the answer cites that record.
4. A finding that holds is one more attempt: through
   `recs-skills-test-driven-development`, one change, compared, kept or
   reverted. It is not an edit made on the reviewer's word.
5. A finding with no record section, or one a record contradicts, is listed
   as open for the record keeper, in one sentence; it may be a record change.

## Never

- "You're right" before the record is open;
- act on findings in a batch before each is checked;
- change a record to make a finding hold.
